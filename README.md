One word for the whole internet. (16 characters)

## The word

> Enshittification

## Why it works

It is the internet's own biography in a single word: platforms that started free and useful, then slowly sold every good part back to the people who built them — the users, the creators, the advertisers — until the whole thing runs on ads, fees and sludge. It is funny because it is rude, and it fits because it is true of the internet as a whole, not of one app or one mood. Nobody's first obvious word, and it lands in one beat.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The word is printed and written to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_PATH`). The same word is also in `entry.txt` at the repository root.
